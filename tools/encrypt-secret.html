Encrypt your OAuth client secret

Runs entirely in your browser (Web Crypto) — nothing is sent anywhere. Enter your secret and a passphrase, then paste the result into clientSecretEnc in js/config.js. You'll type the same passphrase in the app once per session to unlock sync.

Note: This protects the secret at rest in your JS. A determined attacker who has both the ciphertext and your passphrase can recover it. Choose a passphrase you don't use elsewhere. If your deployment is private, plain clientSecret is also an option.
